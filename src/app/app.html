01 — What we have observed

↓

The delay is visible. The cause isn’t.

A treatment request can require a hospital to contact an HMO, wait, follow up repeatedly, and sometimes ask the patient to intervene before a decision is communicated.

What we know

Friction exists across approvals, authorisations, claims, and referrals.

What we don’t know

Whether the root cause is process, policy, operations, technology—or something else.

Why it matters

Patients should not become the follow-up system.
